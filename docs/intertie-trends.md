# Intertie trends: emissions, prices and opening years

Every neighbouring market an intertie reaches carries researched time series in
`src/data/IntertieTrendData.ts`, so imports reflect the era a run is played in rather than one
fixed 2020s snapshot. This replaces the six fixed proxies (and the 445 kg/MWh world-average
fallback most neighbours used) from `ImportEmissions.ts`.

## What each series means

- **Emissions** — generation (production-based) CO2 intensity of the neighbour's grid in
  kg CO2e/MWh, as `[year, value]` anchors: history from about 1980–1990 to 2024, stated-policy
  outlooks (IEA STEPS, national plans, ISO/TSO scenarios) for 2030, 2040 and 2050. The game
  interpolates linearly by fractional game year and holds the ends flat. Most researchers
  reported direct-combustion factors (EIA state data, Canada's NIR Annex, CEA, utility factors,
  Ember/OWID adjusted); some Ember/OWID series use lifecycle factors, which run slightly below
  direct combustion on coal and oil-shale grids (Poland, Estonia). Pre-2000 values are
  frequently estimated from the generation mix.
- **Prices** — annual average wholesale price (day-ahead hub, or for markets without one a
  bulk-supply tariff, export contract or cost-of-supply proxy), nominal USD through 2024 and
  real 2024 USD for 2030+. `intertiePriceIndex` turns it into a multiplier on the market's
  `basePricePerMWh`: the researched price that year over its 2019–2024 average. Before the
  first anchor the record is deflated, and after 2024 the real projection is escalated, both
  at the game's `TREND_ESCALATION_YEARLY` (4%/yr), so intertie prices share the money of the
  fuel prices they compete with.
- **Base prices** — `basePricePerMWh` in `TransmissionProfiles.ts` was reset to the researched
  2019–2024 average, so the price a run sees in a given year is the researched price for that
  year, shaped hourly and seasonally by the market's archetype as before. Many previous
  authored values were far off (Russian markets ~3× high; Balkan, Sahel, Philippine,
  Japanese and New Zealand markets roughly half of reality).
- **Corridor years** — `[availableFromYear, availableUntilYear?]`. For an existing route, the
  year the real interconnection on that path first entered service; for a new route, the
  first year it could plausibly have been ordered (the far-side grid existed and the crossing
  was technically practical). Corridors are hidden from the build list outside that window and
  listed as "Opens later"; lines already built keep operating. No corridor currently needs an
  end year.

## Limits

These are annual averages, not hourly or marginal emissions. Many markets without wholesale
trading use tariff proxies, and all projections are judgement calls; the confidence columns
below say which. Several corridors whose first line predates 1980 carry approximate years,
which has no gameplay effect because the earliest start year is 1980.

The research was produced by a parallel agent survey in September 2026. To revise a market,
edit its series in `IntertieTrendData.ts` and its row below; `src/data/ImportEmissions.test.ts`
checks coverage, ordering and the price-index invariants.
