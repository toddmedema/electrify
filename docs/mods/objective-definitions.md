# Objective definitions

Generated from the evaluator's shared metric definitions.

| Metric                     | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `customerRetention`        | Customers at the deadline divided by the goal's recorded customer baseline.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `emissionsBudget`          | Total electricity and construction emissions over the window, including imports and exports.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `emissionsPerDeliveredMWh` | Total emissions, including imports, exports and construction, per delivered customer MWh over the window.                                                                                                                                                               |
| `localFuelShare`           | Selected fuels' delivered electricity divided by all local fuel-delivered electricity over the window; imports and storage discharge have no fuel provenance.                                                                                                           |
| `fuelRetirement`           | Own no asset of the specified fuel, including paused and unfinished assets, at the deadline and every later month close.                                                                                                                                                |
| `monthlyBilledRate`        | Billed customer revenue per delivered kWh each month, excluding grants and exports.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `monthEndDebt`             | Outstanding loan principal each month, excluding interest.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `utilityOwnership`         | Acquire at least one named utility by the deadline; a completed connection alone does not transfer ownership.                                                                                                                                                           |
| `assetRetirement`          | Retire the named original assets by the deadline; replacement equipment is not targeted.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `financialCoverage`        | Customer and export revenue over the window, or the lowest monthly closing cash, divided by recorded operating expenses including imports, interest and programs; grants, asset purchases and loan principal are excluded. Zero expenses meet the coverage requirement. |
| `customerCount`            | The absolute number of customers at the deadline.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `reserveMargin`            | The lowest recorded reachable supply headroom in every required month, with shortages counted as zero headroom.                                                                                                                                                         |
| `efficiencySavings`        | Measured efficiency energy savings divided by the eligible residential and commercial energy before efficiency over the window.                                                                                                                                         |
| `demandServedShare`        | The lowest monthly share of demand served, across all hours or the specified evening hours; no demand means no unserved demand.                                                                                                                                         |

Objectives may set `checkpoint: true` to judge once at the deadline or window end. Fuel retirement then checks only the deadline month, and an emissions budget is judged only after its accumulation window closes. Omit the flag to preserve the usual metric timing.
