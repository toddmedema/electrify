# Regional maps

The regional map centers on the city selected for the run. The map shows approximate geography and the player's simulated facilities. Plant sites, wires and land cover are schematic. Utility labels identify a local reference or the regional system that inspired the scenario. Across the Valley keeps its fictional utilities and acquisition board around Burlington and Lake Champlain.

The fixed water layer samples [Natural Earth 1:10m land, lakes and rivers](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/) at the same coordinates the [weather catalogue](../public/data/weather/index.json) uses. Tile centers are spaced 10 km apart, with north toward the top of the map. The bundled atlas is about 40 KB and contains water samples and coordinates. The map adds local relief and smaller waterways from the sources below. For other locations it uses catalogue elevation for the surrounding terrain and the existing [data-center utility research](data-center-customers.md) for a verified local reference when its date fits the run. Unresearched locations use a city-named model grid. An embedded location that declares a resource can use a schematic river or a shore oriented toward a vetted offshore weather point.

A local account reference supplies the map label from its published observation year. Undated references use 2026, the year of the customer research. Where utility histories are documented, the map uses their chronology. London starts show the London Electricity Board in 1980, EDF Energy Networks in 2005, and UK Power Networks from 2010. [Data center customer research](data-center-customers.md), [UK Power Networks history](https://www.ukpowernetworks.co.uk/our-company/our-history), [London Electricity Board archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/accessions/2000/00returns/00ac74.htm), [1990 London Electricity record](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1319/schedule/made)

The seed and facility IDs place ordinary plants on distinct sites. Local dams and offshore wind use water. A named site outside the window is represented in its general direction near the edge. Some existing plants require a schematic water plot because their site falls between samples or outside the local window. Chernobyl's four historical reactor units share a compact site north of Kyiv in the regional schematic. Links trace representative connections to the home city. Facility commissioning, dispatch, construction and maintenance use the live game. The procedure also displays old saves without assigning new property to a utility or altering an operating plant.

## Official locations

- **San Francisco** sits on a hilly peninsula between the Pacific and San Francisco Bay. Pacific Gas and Electric is the distribution reference from 1905. Earlier Legacy generations use the city's model grid label. [National Park Service geography](https://www.nps.gov/goga/learn/nature/hydrologicactivity.htm), [California State Parks PG&E history](https://ohp.parks.ca.gov/pages/1067/files/CA_Multiple_PGE%20Historic%20Era%20Electrical%20Infrastructure%20MPDF%20DRAFT.pdf), [San Francisco electricity programs](https://www.sfpuc.gov/programs/clean-energy)
- **Pittsburgh** lies where the Allegheny and Monongahela join to form the Ohio River. Duquesne Light Company is the historic distribution reference. [U.S. Army Corps of Engineers](https://www.lrd.usace.army.mil/pittsburgh/), [Duquesne Light's regional history](https://newsroom.duquesnelight.com/powering-pittsburgh-through-generations)
- **Honolulu** lies on Oʻahu's south shore below the Koʻolau and Waiʻanae ranges. Hawaiian Electric serves the island in the 2003 scenario. [Hawaiʻi Tourism Authority geography](https://www.gohawaii.com/islands/oahu/travel-info/maps), [State of Hawaiʻi 1991–2004 utility data, table 17.13](https://files.hawaii.gov/dbedt/economic/databook/db2004/section17.pdf)
- **San Juan** sits around the entrance to San Juan Bay on Puerto Rico's north coast. The Puerto Rico Electric Power Authority is the 2000 utility reference. LUMA Energy operates the transmission and distribution system from 2021. [National Park Service San Juan geography](https://www.nps.gov/articles/sanjuan.htm), [Puerto Rico Electric Power Authority Act](https://energia.pr.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2025/03/83-1941-2.pdf), [Puerto Rico Energy Bureau filing on the handover](https://energia.pr.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2025/05/20250514-MI20190016-Motion-Subm-Interconnections.pdf)
- **Manassas** is in the Virginia Piedmont near Bull Run, with the Potomac farther east. The city operates its own Electric Department. [National Park Service Manassas geology](https://www.nps.gov/mana/learn/nature/geology.htm), [City of Manassas electric program](https://cms9files.revize.com/manassasva/Finance/CIP/2025/Section%2008%20-%20Electric.pdf)
- **Austin** follows the Colorado River where the Texas Hill Country gives way to lower terrain to the east. Austin Energy is its city utility. [Lower Colorado River Authority](https://www.lcra.org/water/flood-management/), [Austin Energy service area](https://austinenergy.com/about/company-profile/electric-system/service-area-map), [Austin Energy history](https://austinenergy.com/about/history)
- **Madrid** lies east of the Manzanares with the Sierra de Guadarrama to its northwest. UFD Distribución Electricidad serves parts of the city and is the map's local reference for the Spain-inspired scenario. [City of Madrid river works](https://diario.madrid.es/programambientales/renaturalizacion-del-manzanares/), [Community of Madrid on Guadarrama](https://www.comunidad.madrid/centros/guadarrama), [Naturgy's UFD launch](https://www.naturgy.com/notas-de-prensa/naturgy-lanza-ufd-la-nueva-marca-para-su-negocio-de-distribucion-de-electricidad-en-espana/), [Madrid distribution project](https://www.comunidad.madrid/transparencia/2024p655-proyecto-ejecucion-instalacion-linea-subterranea-circuito-casa-campo-manuel-becerra-madrid)
- **Paris** is on the Seine. Enedis distributes electricity under a city concession. The historical names follow the distributor's chronology. [City of Paris geography](https://www.paris.fr/pages/la-seine-aurait-plus-de-14-000-ans-et-autres-anecdotes-surprenantes-sur-le-fleuve-parisien-19981), [City concession](https://presse.paris.fr/communiques/9671), [Enedis history](https://www.enedis.fr/enedis-en-bref/notre-histoire)
- **Los Angeles** lies between the Pacific coast and the Santa Monica and San Gabriel mountains. Los Angeles Department of Water and Power is the municipal utility. [National Park Service geography](https://home.nps.gov/articles/nps-geodiversity-atlas-santa-monica-mountains-national-recreation-area-california.htm), [LADWP utility history](https://www.ladwp.com/who-we-are/our-history/power-past-present)
- **Johannesburg** sits on the inland Witwatersrand ridge. Load Shedding represents part of Eskom's national supply. The same city's custom map references City Power Johannesburg. [City environmental report](https://joburg.org.za/documents_/Documents/Growth%20Management%20Strategy/BUDGET%202003-04/STATE%20OF%20THE%20ENVIRONMENT.pdf), [City Power](https://joburg.org.za/departments_/municipalownedentities/Pages/city-power.aspx), [Eskom and City Power responsibilities](https://www.eskom.co.za/eskom-and-city-power-sign-memorandum-of-understanding-on-possible-transfer-of-johannesburg-electricity-distribution-to-city-power/)
- **Lusaka** stands on a plateau north of the Kafue and Zambezi. The River Runs Dry models ZESCO supply, with major dams outside the city. [Lusaka City Council](https://lusakacitycouncil.co.zm/lusaka/), [ZESCO](https://www.zesco.co.zm/about), [Zambezi River Authority on Kariba](https://www.zambezira.org/node/20)
- **Delhi** lies west of the Yamuna, with the Aravalli-derived Delhi Ridge to the west and south. The city label references the BSES and Tata Power-DDL distribution networks. [Delhi Forest Department ridge guide](https://forest.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/generic_multiple_files/an_introduction_to_the_delhi_ridge.pdf), [City Yamuna floodplain information](https://ifc.delhi.gov.in/ifc/flood-problem-due-river-yamuna), [Delhi distribution licenses](https://www.derc.gov.in/license-issued-to-utilities)
- **Burlington** stands on the eastern shore of Lake Champlain. Burlington Electric Department is the historical and modern utility reference for custom games there. [City of Burlington waterfront geography](https://www.burlingtonvt.gov/1048/Greenway-Multi-Use-Path), [Burlington Electric history](https://www.burlingtonelectric.com/history/)
- **Kyiv** lies on the Dnipro. The 1986 scenario retains its Ukrainian SSR setting and references Kyivenergo for the city. The Chornobyl plant site is near the Pripyat river, beyond the local city window. [City of Kyiv Dnipro guide](https://guide.kyivcity.gov.ua/places/richka-dnipro), [Historical Kyivenergo](https://kievenergo.com.ua/en/about-company/company/), [Modern distributor transition](https://reforma.dtek.com/en/publications-about-the-reform/s-1-marta-potrebiteley-elektroenergii-kieva-udet-obsluzhivat-chao-dtek-kievskie-elektroseti/), [Chornobyl plant siting](https://gallery.chnpp.gov.ua/en/41-about/building/423-2010-09-08-09-57-41423)

## Updating the water atlas

Download `ne_10m_land.geojson`, `ne_10m_lakes.geojson` and `ne_10m_rivers_lake_centerlines.geojson` from the pinned [Natural Earth v5.1.2 GeoJSON release](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson) into one directory. After changing the weather catalogue, regenerate the water samples from the repository root.

```sh
node scripts/generate-regional-water.js /path/to/natural-earth-geojson
```

The generator writes `src/data/RegionalWater.json`. A change to capacity shares or starting facilities needs no geography regeneration.
