About Electrify

You run an electric utility. Can you cut emissions and still keep the lights on when everyone gets home?

Play Electrify Read the source code

Where the numbers come from

A power plant can be cheap to build and expensive to run. In Electrify, you choose the plants and storage your customers depend on, then see what it costs to meet demand.

The game uses research on power markets and generation technologies. You’ll find the references in the Electrify source code on GitHub. You can play for free in your browser.

Playing and teaching

  • Run an island grid, prepare for a deep freeze, or build a utility across nine generations in the official scenarios.
  • Learn how to play, from reading the forecast to choosing your first power plant.
  • Bring Electrify to your classroom with a lesson plan and printable student worksheet.
  • Compare energy and climate simulators for modeling a utility, a region, or the whole planet.
  • Compare Electrify and En-ROADS to see how running a utility differs from testing global climate policy.
