Electrify can help you understand the impacts of potential data center projects in your community.

An example: adding 500 MW

Here’s what happens if you add a 500 MW data center to Austin, TX in 2027 in Electrify:

No new campus 500 MW campus
Smallest reserve No new campus680 MW → 500 MW campus180 MW (−73%)
Fuel and running costs No new campus$450 million → 500 MW campus$520 million (+17%)
Emissions No new campus6.1 million tonnes CO₂e → 500 MW campus7.2 million tonnes CO₂e (+17%)

Try it yourself

  1. Open the data-center setup. Choose Austin, set Start year to 2026 and Data centers open to 2027.
  2. Under How much extra power?, set 0 MW and choose Start exploring.
  3. Play until 2028 or later to see the impact, then repeat with 500 MW.

The new campus draws constant power, while the game uses one representative day per month. This exercise cannot forecast a real project’s reliability or local bills.

Take these questions to your community

  • Which power plants and grid upgrades must be ready when the campus opens?
  • Who will pay for the new infrastructure and expenses?
  • Where does the power come from on a windless night, and how does a clean-energy promise account for that?
