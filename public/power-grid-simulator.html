Run your own power grid

The sun is going down and demand is climbing. Will your grid get through the evening? In Electrify, you’re in charge of the power plants, the electricity price, and the next decision.

Build a grid you can afford to keep running

Choose power plants and storage, set electricity prices, and decide when to borrow for the next project. A cheap plant may need expensive fuel. A wind farm’s output changes with the weather. A battery can cover a shortage, if you charged it in time. Plan ahead: a plant under construction cannot help with tonight’s shortage. Check the forecast and leave yourself time to respond.

Start with one day. Then take on a bigger grid.

Play free in your browser on a computer, tablet, or phone. No account is needed. Choose Play to start the tutorials, then take on a scenario or create your own game. You can pause to inspect the grid and speed time up when you’re ready.

Try a plan and see what happens

Electrify uses one representative day per month. It’s a game about energy tradeoffs, not a forecast of a real project’s costs or reliability.

Playing with a group? Try the classroom lesson. Curious about growing demand? Explore data centers and your grid.
