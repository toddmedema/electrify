Electrify privacy

Back to the game

Electrify is playable without an account. Your current game and preferences are saved in this browser on your device.

Optional online features

If you sign in with Google, Electrify receives your account identifier and basic profile name so you can claim a separate public leaderboard display name. Scores, that display name, and an optional replay of your game actions may be stored in Firebase and shown publicly. Do not choose a display name you want to keep private.

Analytics

Electrify uses aggregate usage analytics to understand which screens and missions are used and where errors occur. Shared links use simple campaign parameters; the game does not use fingerprinting.

Your choices

You can play without signing in, clear local game data through your browser, and contact todd@fabricate.io to ask about leaderboard data or deletion.
