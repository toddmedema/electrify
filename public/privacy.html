Your data and your choices

Privacy, in plain language.

Play without an account. Keep your game on your device. Choose if you want to join the public leaderboard.

Last updated September 1, 2026

Your game stays in your browser

Electrify is playable without an account. Your current game and preferences are saved in this browser on your device.

Optional online features

If you sign in with Google, Electrify receives your account identifier and basic profile name so you can claim a separate public leaderboard display name. Scores, that display name, and an optional replay of your game actions may be stored in Firebase and shown publicly. Do not choose a display name you want to keep private.

Analytics

Electrify uses aggregate usage analytics to understand which screens and missions are used and where errors occur. Shared links use simple campaign parameters; the game does not use fingerprinting.

Email updates

If you subscribe to launch updates, your email address is submitted to Mailchimp and used only to send those updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in an email.

Your choices

You can play without signing in, clear local game data through your browser, and use the feedback form to ask about leaderboard data or deletion.

At a glance

  • No account is required to play.
  • Game progress and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Leaderboard participation and replays are optional.
  • Email updates are optional and you can unsubscribe anytime.
  • You can request deletion of leaderboard data.
