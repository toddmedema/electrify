The design inference was to combine a dominant recognizable title with a clear
electricity motif. Electrify's chart-and-facility decisions support a restrained
graphic composition. Scenic geography and explicit wire-routing artwork would
suggest interactions that the gameplay screenshots do not show. Competitor event
ribbons or legacy taglines were not treated as exceptions to Valve's current rules.
